# Field of Flame \[Formulae\] \[Fire\] \[Contact\]
Tier 1. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Fire 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Delay]] 1. [[Cooldown]] 4. 
[[Area Burst]] 2 within 10.
Effect: The Burst becomes a [[Zone]] with 3 charges. Creatures that enter the Zone or start their phase in it suffer [[Fire]] 3.
At the end of the Autonomous phase, remove 1 charge from the Zone. Then, if it has no charges, it ends.
[[Contact]]: For 1 action, you may reduce the current cooldown of this Formulae by 1.

# Ranged Attack \[Basic\]
1 action
Effect: Decide if you wish to attack within the first or second range of your Weapon. If you choose the second, you must succeed at a Power test or your damage will be halved.  Roll damage, and assign it to any number of targets in range within your facing. You may split the damage total however you wish.

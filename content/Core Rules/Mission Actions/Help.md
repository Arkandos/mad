# Help \[Basic\]
1 action
Effect: You attempt to help an ally with a [[Core Rules|Test]] they are about to perform. Generally you need to be adjacent to them, but certain situations may require you to be in a different position. Until the end of the Mage Phase, that ally gains a +2d bonus to that type of Test.

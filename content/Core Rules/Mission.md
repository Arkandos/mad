While deployed to a Mission, time is of the essence. The enemy is innumerable and you are a heroic few. It is therefore of utmost importance to complete the mission objectives as quickly as possible before you are overrun.

Each Mission has one or more objectives that must be completed for you to be able to win. As a cooperative game, the Cabal of Mages wins or loses together.

# Damage, Injuries and Dying

## Injuries
When you take damage that reduces you to 5 toughness or below*, you suffer a devastating Injury. Roll 1d8 and consult the following chart (which you may reroll using Mythos). If you would receive an Injury you already possess, instead reroll the Injury.

A roll of 1-4 is a Critical Injury and cannot normally be cured. Non-critical Injuries are cured by regaining any amount of Toughness. Unless otherwise removed, injuries last until the end of the Mission.
1. **Head** - At the start of your turn, you become [[Staggered]].
2. **Haemorrhage** - At the start of your turn, you lose 3 Toughness
3. **Arm lost** - You can only use one Weapon and cannot [[Channel]].
4. **Leg lost** - You are [[Prone]] and cannot [[Stand up]].
5. **Hand lost** - You lose 1 Toughness whenever you use an action to [[Channel]], cast a Spell or Formulae.
6. **Arm injured** - You cannot [[Focus]], or affect targets beyond the first Range increment. Your Melee Weapons do not add your Might to their damage.
7. **Leg injured** - Your [[Speed]] is reduced by 2.
8. **The Weave protects** - You are miraculously unharmed. Mostly.

\* When playing at Hard difficulty, you also suffer an Injury when you take damage that reduces you to 10 toughness or below. If one damage instance would reduce you from above 10 to 5 or below, you receive 2 Injuries.

# Doom Clock
At the end of every round, the Doom Clock increases by 1 [[Doom]]. Once it has Doom equal to the missions Doom limit, the game ends in defeat unless otherwise specified. At certain Doom thresholds, mission effects may trigger that hinder the Cabal.

When you are reduced to 0 Toughness, you are Defeated. When a creature is Defeated, they are removed from the game. 

When your mage is Defeated, resolve the following steps:
1. Place all Weapons and all Equipment in your space. Then remove your Mage from the game.
2. Reroll all your Skills as detailed in [[Mage Creation]] (step 1)
3. Keep track of your Formulae Cooldowns & Uses, and Mythos points. Reset your Toughness and Weave to their maximum values.
A Defeated Mage returns in one of three ways: 
4. Another Mage using the [[Mage's Convocation]] Formulae
5. At the end of the Mage phase, if you are still Defeated place your Mage within 5 squares of another Mage. Set your Weave to 0.
6. At the start of the Mage phase, if all Mages are Defeated, place all Mages within 5 squares of where the last Mage died. Set your Weave to 0.

Once you have grasped the [[Core Rules]], you are ready to make a Mage.

1. Roll 5d6 and remove the lowest die. Assign the other dice, one for each [[Mage Details#Skills|Skill]]. If your total scores in all skills are 12 or below, scrap them and roll again.
2. Then choose two Tier 0 [[Spellbook/Techniques/index|Techniques]] and two Tier 0 [[Spellbook/Forms/index|Forms]]. Together they are how you create [[Spells]].
3. Choose two different [[Affinities]] to gain Rank 1 in, and then choose one Tier 1 [[Formulae]]. You gain the [[Mage's Convocation]] Formulae for free.
4. [[Formulae]] have prerequisites of a certain Affinity Rank that you need to fulfil to be able to acquire them. **Content without listed prerequisites do not require Affinities**
5. After that you are ready to play your first [[Mission]]. When preparing for it, choose 1 [[Armor (equipment)|Armor]] and up to 2 [[Weapons]]

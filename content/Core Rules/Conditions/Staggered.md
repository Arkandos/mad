While you are Staggered, you cannot restore your own [[Toughness]], you lose the benefits of Focus, and take a -2d penalty on any [[Core Rules|Test]] you make. You may spend 1 action to end this status.
At the end of your turn, you lose Staggered.

Staggered enemies can only move or take an action, not both.

Some allies (that aren’t one of your Mages) have the unfortunate downside of becoming Terrified in stressful situations. This is a special status effect that makes them act unpredictably, often freezing in place or running away. A Terrified creature will not obey orders given by a Mage.
A Mage within 10 squares of a Terrified creature may attempt to calm them down with an Intelligence roll, or a Might roll if adjacent to them. Once a number of successes equal to the creatures Terrified value have been achieved, remove the Terrified status.

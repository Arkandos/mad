title: Magic and Damnation

*Brave mages of the nine realms, we call upon you to save us from the forces of darkness! Only your mystical might may maintain magistrates manifestation!*
*By Arkandos*

Magic and Damnation is a turn based cooperative tactical boardgame, played by 2-6 players, one of which takes on the role of the Magistrate who controls the enemies. The other players take on the role of Mages who seek to grow in power and save the last realms from the descending darkness.

In their journey for power, they  come upon the temptations of Damnation. What's a little corruption, if it grants you the power to keep going?

During missions, the [[Mage Details|Mage]]s take turns moving around the board and slinging spells at friends and foes in the hopes of completing objectives before the [[Mission#Doom Clock|Doom]] Clock tolls. Between each mission, the Mages can spent experience earned to improve their character and learn new powerful magical abilities.

Begin by reading the [[Core Rules]] before proceeding to [[Mage Creation]] to prepare for your first [[Mission]]!

*While some lesser minds would not call a robe and hat ‘armor’, the true Mage knows that their protective runes are just as powerful as any armor.*
When preparing for a [[Mission]], choose one of the following Armors to equip.

# Light
## Lofty robe
+1d [[Armor]], +1 [[Speed]], +2d [[Resist]]

# Medium
## Heavy robe & hat
+3d [[Armor]], +1d [[Resist]], +1 Intelligence

# Heavy
## Magical Armor
+6d [[Armor]], +1 Might, -1 Power except when using Weapons, -1 [[Weave]] when [[Channel|Channelling]]

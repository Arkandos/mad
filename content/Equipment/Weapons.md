*As a Mage, you might require a weapon in certain situations where you do not want to use your precious Weave. But beware, non-magical weapons such as these are only a crutch and not something to be relied upon. For those who wish to wield weapons of true magick might, look to Formulae.*
When preparing for a [[Mission]], choose two of the following Weapons to equip.

# Melee
## Staff \[Weapon\] \[Melee\]
Melee 2, 1d8 Damage, Pierce 3
While this Weapon is active, increase your Melee range by 1 for [[Spells]] and [[Formulae]]
## Sword \[Weapon\] \[Melee\]
Melee 2, 1d6 Damage, Pierce 3
While this Weapon is active and you make a Melee Attack using it, you may move 1 additional square.
# Ranged
## Crossbow \[Weapon\] \[Ranged\]
Ranged 20/40, 2d4 Damage, Pierce 3
Damage must be assigned to a single target.
## Shortbow \[Weapon\] \[Ranged\]
Ranged 10/20, 1d6 Damage, Pierce 3

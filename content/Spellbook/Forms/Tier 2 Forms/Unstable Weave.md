# Unstable Weave \[Form\] \[Esoteric\] \[Link\] \[Support\]
Form Tier 2. Rank 1.
Effect: All targets gain 2 Weave. Then at the start of the next Mage Phase, they must pay 3 Weave. For every Weave they cannot pay, they gain 1 [[Weave Burn]].

# Awaken Elemental \[Form\] \[Elemental\] \[Link\] \[Summon\]
Form Tier 2. Rank 1.
1 action. Weave 4.
Effect: Choose an Elemental keyword in this Spell. At the start of the next Mage Phase, Summon a [[Elemental, Small|Small Elemental]] with the chosen Affinity in one of the targeted squares.
It is [[Aggressive]].
[[Elemental]]: Replace the Elemental keyword of this part with an Elemental keyword in this Spell.
[[Summon]]: You may only have 1 Summon active at once.

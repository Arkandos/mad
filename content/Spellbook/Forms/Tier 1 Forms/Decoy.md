# Decoy \[Form\] \[Illusion\] \[Link\] \[Support\]
Form Tier 1. Rank 1.
Weave 2.
Effect: Place a Decoy Token in one of the targeted squares. Non-Champion Enemies consider it as a Mage for the purposes of targeting and moving, and may deal damage to it. Once it takes any damage, it ends.
You may only have 1 Decoy active at once.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
You may have an additional Decoy active at once, and they only end when they have taken (5 + Illusion Ranks) Damage.
## Rank 3
Weave 3.
Once a Decoy Token ends, all creatures within 2 squares of it become [[Fleeing]].

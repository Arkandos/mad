# Shift Senses \[Form\] \[Perceive\] \[Link\] \[Sense\]
Form Tier 1. Rank 1.
Weave 1.
Effect: Choose a square in the targeted area and place a Sense token there until the end of the next Mage Phase. You can see everything the token sees (this does not change your line of sight).

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
At the end of every Mage Phase, you may spend 1 Weave to have this effect last until the next Mage Phase.

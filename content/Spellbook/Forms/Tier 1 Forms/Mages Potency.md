# Mages Potency \[Form\] \[Esoteric\] \[Link\] \[Support\] \[Teamwork\]
Form Tier 1. Rank 1.
Weave 2.
Effect: Choose 1 targeted Mage other than yourself. They gain a +2 bonus on all damage rolls until the end of the Mage Phase. They cannot benefit from Mages Potency again until the end of the Mage Phase.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
They gain a +(2 + your Esoteric ranks) bonus on all damage rolls (maximum 4).
## Rank 3
Choose 1 upgrade:
* Reduce the Weave cost by 1, and increase the maximum bonus to 5.
* Choose up to 2 targeted mages other than yourself.

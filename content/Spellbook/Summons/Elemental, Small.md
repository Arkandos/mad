# Small Elemental \[Elemental\] \[Summon\]
Medium Elemental. Toughness 10. Armor 6. Speed 6.
▶ Melee 1, Damage 2d6+4 (Pierce 3), applied to lowest toughness creatures first.

Gains additional effects depending on the associated Affinity:
* Air: Speed increased to 8. Can fly.
* Earth: Speed reduced by 2. Armor increased to 10.
* Fire: Attack splits damage equally between all targets, and applies [[Fire]] 3.
* Water: Attack is now Ranged 5, and applies [[Slowed]] 2.
[[Summon]]: You may only have 1 Summon active at once.

# Bonded Mount \[Beast\] \[Nature\] \[Summon\]
Large Beast. Toughness 10. Armor 6. Speed 8.
* Whenever the rider takes damage from an effect you are not targeted by, you also take an equal amount (after rolling Armor but before applying Block). You do not roll Armor against that damage. While you have a rider, you can only move once per Autonomous phase.
[[Summon]]: You may only have 1 Summon active at once.

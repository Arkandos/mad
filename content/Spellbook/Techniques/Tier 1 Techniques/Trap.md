# Trap \[Technique\] \[Rune\] \[Opener\] \[Targeting\]
Tier 1. Rank 1.
1 action. 1 Weave.
[[Area Burst]] 1 Zone within 5/15.
Effect: Choose up to 3 \[Offensive\] \[Links\]. Roll and record their damage now.
Once a creature enters the Zone, you must detonate the Zone before the end of that Phase. When you do all creatures in the Zone take that damage (Groups and Hordes take 50% more) but ignore any other effect of attached \[Links\]. Then the Zone ends.

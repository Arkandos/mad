# Scry \[Technique\] \[Perceive\] \[Opener\] \[Targeting\]
Tier 1. Rank 1.
1 actions, Weave 2.
[[Area Burst]] 2 within 20/40 which becomes a Zone.
Effect: Until the end of the next Mage Phase, the Zone is affected by up to 3 of your \[Sense\] \[Links\].
Special: You do not need line of sight or effect to the targeted area.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
When targeting a square within the first range, increase the size of the Burst by 1.
## Rank 3
You may increase the Weave cost by 2 to increase the duration by 1 round.

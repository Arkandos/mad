# Cascade \[Technique\] \[Create\] \[Link\] \[Targeting\] \[Modifier\]
Technique Tier 1. Rank 1.
Weave 3.
[[Modifier|Modifies]]: A previous \[Targeting\] part of the Spell that only targets 1 creature.
Effect: After the Spell has resolved all parts between the Modified part and this part, repeat those parts against the nearest creature within 10 squares of the previous target. Repeat this effect 3 additional times, but no creature can be targeted by this Spell more than once.

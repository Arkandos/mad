# Torrent \[Technique\] \[Create\] \[Opener\] \[Targeting\]
Tier 1. Rank 1.
2 actions, Weave 2.
[[Area Burst]] 1 within 20/40, all creatures.
Effect: The targets are affected by up to 3 of your \[Offensive\] or \[Hex\] \[Links\].
All targets take the Damage, Groups and Hordes take 50% more.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
When targeting a square within the first range, you may increase the size of the Burst by 1.
## Rank 3
You may spend 1 additional action and 1 Weave to increase the size of the Burst by 1.

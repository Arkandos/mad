# Roaming \[Technique\] \[Enchantment\] \[Opener\] \[Targeting\]
Technique Tier 3. Rank 1.
2 actions. Weave 1.
Ranged 5, 1 unoccupied square.
Effect: The targeted area becomes a [[Zone]] until the end of the Mission, or you use this Technique again. At the start of the Autonomous Phase, you must pay 1 Weave or the Zone ends. Then you may move the Zone 4 squares, affecting all creatures it moves over with 1 of your \[Links\] (paying its cost).

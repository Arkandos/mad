# Barrage \[Technique\] \[Create\] \[Opener\] \[Targeting\]
Technique Tier 0. Rank 1.
1 action. Weave 1.
Ranged 15/25, any number of creatures in one facing.
Effect: The target is affected by up to 3 of your \[Offensive\] \[Links\].
You may split the Damage between them as you wish. If you target only a single Group or Horde, it takes 50% more damage.

Special: Only targets that take Damage from this Spell are affected by its non-damage effects.

# Reverberate \[Technique\] \[Esoteric\] \[Surge\] \[Modifier\]
Technique Tier 2. Rank 1.
Weave 1.
[[Modifier|Modifies]]: A Spell that only targets a single allied creature.
Effect: At the start of the next Mage Phase, repeat this Spell on the same target (it cannot repeat again). Then, if your Weave is lower than the Weave cost of all your Spells with Reverberate that triggered this round, you are [[Stunned]] 1.

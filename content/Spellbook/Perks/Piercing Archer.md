# Piercing Archer \[Perk\] \[Perceive\] \[Weapon\]
Rank 1. XP 2. Prerequisite: Perceive 1.
After you use the [[Ranged Attack]] action, choose one target. Another creature within 5 squares of them but further away from you takes half the damage the target took. The secondary creature cannot have been targeted by the Ranged Attack.

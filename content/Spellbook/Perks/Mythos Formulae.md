# Mythos Formulae \[Perk\] \[Esoteric\]
Rank 1. XP 2. Prerequisite: Esoteric 1.
When you spend a Mythos point to reroll the Test to use a Formulae, you may roll twice and use the higher number of successes. If you still fail, gain 1 Mythos point.

# Spellsniper \[Perk\] \[Esoteric\]
Rank 1. XP 2. Prerequisite: Esoteric 1.
Whenever you use a Formulae or Spell, if you haven’t moved since the start of your last turn:
- Increase the range of Ranged and [[Area Burst|Area Bursts]] you create by 5.
- Increase the length of Cones and [[Line|Lines]] you create by 5.
- Increase the size of your [[Close Burst|Close Bursts]] by 3.

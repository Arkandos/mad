# Natural Weaver \[Perk\] \[Nature/Elemental\]
Rank 1. XP 4. Prerequisite: Nature or Elemental 2.
At the end of your turn, if you used only \[Forms\] and Formulae that contain Nature or Elemental keywords, gain 1 Weave. You must have used at least one Spell or Formulae to trigger this perk.

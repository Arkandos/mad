# Liquid Essence \[Perk\] \[Water\]
Rank 1. XP 4. Prerequisite: Water 2.
After you use a \[Water\] Formulae or Spell, place 1 Water Essence on all enemy targets.
When an enemy with Water Essence is Defeated, take control of the Essence. You may spend Water Essence as if it was Weave for \[Water\] effects.
You may hold a maximum of 3 Water Essence at once.

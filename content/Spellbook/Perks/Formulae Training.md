# Formulae Training \[Perk\]
Rank 1. XP 2.
Reduce the action cost of the first Formulae you use each Mission by 1.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
XP 2.
Reduce the Weave cost of the first Formulae you use each Mission by 3.

## Rank 3
XP 2.
You become [[Stunned]] 3 at the start of a Mission. During your first turn, all Formulae you use gain the following benefits:
Their action cost is reduced by 2.
Their Weave cost is reduced by 3.
Score 1 automatic success on the Intelligence test to use them.

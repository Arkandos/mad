# Mobile Striker \[Perk\] \[Body\] \[Weapon\]
Rank 1. XP 2. Prerequisite: Body 1
When you use the [[Melee Attack]] action, you can choose any point along the movement to deal the damage.
When you use the [[Run and Shoot]] action, you gain a +2d bonus to the Power test.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
XP 2.
When you use the Melee Attack action, roll damage before moving. After moving, you may split the damage as you wish between all targets you moved adjacent to.

When you use the Ranged Attack action, you may target at the second range, but you require an additional success.

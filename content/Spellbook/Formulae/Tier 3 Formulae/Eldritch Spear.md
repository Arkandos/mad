# Eldritch Spear \[Formulae\] \[Weapon\] \[Create\] \[Enchantment\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Enchantment 2, Create 1. 
1 action. 5 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 1 Mission.
Effect: You gain the following Weapon until the end of the Mission:

## Eldritch Spear \[Weapon\] \[Melee\] \[Summoned\]
Range 2. 2d6 damage. Pierce 3. Charges 0.
When making a Melee Attack with this Weapon, instead of targeting all enemies within one facing, place a [[Line]] 1 by 2 within range and target all creatures in the Line.
You may spend a charge from this Weapon per target to make them [[Sunder|Sundered]] 1 until the end of the Mage Phase.

At the start of each Mage Phase, the Weapon gains 1 Charge. You may spend any amount of Weave to gain half that many additional charges.

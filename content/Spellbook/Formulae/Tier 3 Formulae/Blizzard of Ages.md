# Blizzard of Ages \[Formulae\] \[Create\] \[Water\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisites: Water 2, Create 1.
3 actions. 2 Weave. 2 successes. [[Delay]] 1. [[Cooldown]] 5.
[[Area Burst]] 1 [[Zone]] within 20/40.
Effect: At the start of every Mage Phase, roll 2d6+6 Damage (Pierce 2). Any creature that enters the Zone, or starts their turn in it takes that amount of damage and is [[Slowed]] 3.
In the Autonomous phase, increase the size of the Zone by 1 (as if it was a Burst) up to a maximum size of Burst 5. Then, you gain 1 Weave Burn per Burst size of the Zone.
You may end this effect for 1 action.

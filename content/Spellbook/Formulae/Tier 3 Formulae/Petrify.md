# Petrify \[Formulae\] \[Transmute\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Transmute 2.
1 action. Weave 2. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 4.
Ranged 10/20, 1 Standard enemy.
Effect: The target is [[Slowed]] 3. At the start of the next Mage Phase, they are [[Defeated]], and an [[Obstruction]] with 20 Toughness replaces their space.

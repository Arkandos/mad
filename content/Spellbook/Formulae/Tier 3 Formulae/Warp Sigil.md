# Warp Sigil \[Formulae\] \[Control\] \[Rune\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisites: Control 2, Rune 1.
1 action. 3 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 3.
Melee 1, 1 unoccupied square.
Effect: The target square becomes a [[Zone]] until the end of the Mission. Whenever an ally enters the Zone, they may [[teleport]] to any other Warp Sigil Zone you have created.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
Choose 1 upgrade:
* Increase the range to Ranged 5, 1 unoccupied square.
* Whenever a Standard enemy is forcefully moved into one of the Zones, you may teleport them to another.

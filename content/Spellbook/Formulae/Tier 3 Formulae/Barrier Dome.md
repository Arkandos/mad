# Barrier Dome \[Formulae\] \[Ward\] \[Rune\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisites: Ward 2, Rune 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 2 successes. [[Cooldown]] 4.
[[Close Burst]] 5 [[Zone]]
Effect: Creatures in the Zone have [[Block]] 10 and ignore status effects from non-Champion creatures outside the Zone.
The Zone has 2 charges, and loses a charge at the end of every Mage Phase.
While you are in the Zone, you may for 1 action add a charge to the Zone.

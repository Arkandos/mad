# Last Hope Aegis \[Formulae\] \[Ward\] \[Body\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Ward 2, Body 1.
Reaction. 2 Weave. [[Cooldown]] 4.
Trigger: An ally within 20 squares would be [[Defeated]] by damage.
Effect: The ally is not Defeated and remains at 1 Toughness instead. You lose Toughness equal to the excess damage.
Special: You do not need line of sight or effect to the target, but you must be aware of them.

# Phantom Wizard \[Formulae\] \[Perceive\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 3. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Perceive 2, Illusion 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 4.
Effect: Place a Sense [[Token]] adjacent to you, then move it up to 15 squares. It provides vision. You may [[Interact]], [[Study]], and use \[Perceive\] \[Openers\] as if you were the Sense Token.
At the start of the Mage Phase, you may move it an additional 10 squares.
At the end of the next Mage Phase, the Sense Token ends.

# Upgrades
## Rank 2
You may increase the Weave cost by 2 to increase the duration by an additional Mage Phase.

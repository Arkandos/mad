# Gust of Wind \[Formulae\] \[Air\]
Tier 1. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Air 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Delay]] 0. [[Cooldown]] 5.
[[Line]] 3 squares wide, 8 squares long placed within 10 squares.
Effect: The Line becomes a [[Zone]] with 2 charges. Choose one side of the Zone to be its start. Moving through the Zone towards its start counts as difficult terrain.
At the start of the Autonomous Phase, all creatures in the Zone are pushed 5 squares away from its start. Then remove 1 charge from the Zone, and if it has 0 charges it ends.

# Warding Ring \[Formulae\] \[Ward\]
Tier 1. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Ward 1.
2 actions. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 8.
[[Close Burst]] 5 which becomes a [[Zone]] with 3 charges.
Effect: The Zone is difficult terrain to enemies. When an enemy would enter the Zone, make an Intellect [[Core Rules|Test]]. If you succeed, they cannot enter the Zone this round (2 successes needed for an Elite or Champion enemy). At the start of The Autonomous Phase, remove 1 charge from the Zone. Then if it has 0, it ends.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
When a non-[[Champion]] enemy would attack into or through the Zone, roll as above. If you succeed, the attack deals no Damage.
## Rank 3
Choose 1 upgrade:
- Reduce the targeting to Close Burst 3, but you always gain 1 automatic success on any roll made for this Formulae.
- Increase the Targeting to Close Burst 7, but if you exit the Zone it ends.

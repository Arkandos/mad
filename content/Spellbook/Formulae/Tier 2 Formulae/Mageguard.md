# Mageguard \[Formulae\] \[Enchantment\] \[Summon\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Enchantment 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 2 successes. [[Cooldown]] 10.
Effect: One Weapon you are wielding transforms into an [[Invincible]] [[Summon]] with a Speed equal to your own until the end of the Mission. 
In the Autonomous phase you may move it up to twice your Speed and at any point it may make a Melee or Ranged attack, using your Enchantment score x 2 in place of any Skill.
[[Summon]]: You may only have 1 Summon active at once.

# Inescapable Attraction \[Formulae\] \[Control\] \[Body\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisites: Control 1, Body 1
1 action. 4 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 4.
Ranged 20/40, 2 enemies
Effect: The targets are pulled up to 5 squares towards each other.
Then if they are adjacent to each other, or cannot be pulled closer due to some Obstruction, they take 15 Damage (Pierce 3) and are [[Immobilized]] until the next Mage Phase.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
Repeat the effect at the start of the next Mage Phase, targeting the same enemies (ignoring line of sight). If one is not an available target, you may choose a different creature to affect.
## Rank 3
You may spend an additional action to affect 2 additional enemies. No creature can take the damage more than once.

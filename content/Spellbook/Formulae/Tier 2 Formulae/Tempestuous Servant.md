# Tempestuous Servant \[Formulae\] \[Enchantment\] \[Air\] \[Summon\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Enchantment 1, Air 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 2 successes. [[Cooldown]] 5.
Ranged 10, 1 unoccupied square.
Effect: You [[Summon]] an [[Invincible]] Tempest in the target square with a Speed of 5. In the Autonomous phase, it may move its Speed. It has an [[Aura]] 3, and once per Autonomous phase you may slide all enemies in the Aura 3 squares.
At the end of every Mage Phase, you must pay 1 Weave or the Summon ends.
[[Summon]]: You may only have 1 Summon active at once.

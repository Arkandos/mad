# Hexblade \[Formulae\] \[Enchantment\] \[Esoteric\] \[Imbuement\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Enchantment 1, Esoteric 1.
1 action. 4 Weave. 2 successes. [[Cooldown]] 1.
Effect: Choose one \[Hex\] \[Link\] that you possess. One Weapon you wield gains that \[Hex\] as an Imbuement until the end of the Mission. At the start of your turn, you may pay the cost of the \[Hex\] to have it apply to all targets you damage with the imbued Weapon until the end of your turn.
[[Imbuement]]: A Weapon may only have 1 Imbuement active at once.

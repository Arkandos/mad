# Illusory Wall \[Formulae\] \[Illusion\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Illusion 1.
1 action. 1 Weave. 2 successes. [[Cooldown]] 5.
[[Line]] 1 by 8 placed within 10/20 squares.
Effect: The Line becomes a 3 square high Wall. When an enemy tries to move into or attack through the Wall, roll an Intellect Test. If you succeed, they treat the Wall as an [[Obstruction]]. (2 successes needed for an [[Elite]], 4 for a [[Champion]]). At the start of The Autonomous Phase, you must either become [[Stunned]] 1 or pay 2 Weave, or the Wall ends.

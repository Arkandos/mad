# Field of Frost \[Formulae\] \[Control\] \[Water\] \[Contact\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Control 1, Water 1.
1 action. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Delay]] 1. [[Cooldown]] 5. 
[[Area Burst]] 2 within 10.
Effect: The Burst becomes a [[Zone]] with 2 charges. Creatures that enter the Zone or start their phase in it are [[Slowed]] 2. Non-[[Champion]] creatures that were already Slowed are instead [[Staggered]] for 1 round.
At the end of the Autonomous phase, remove 1 charge from the Zone. Then, if it has no charges, it ends.
[[Contact]]: For 1 action, you may reduce the current cooldown of this Formulae by 1.

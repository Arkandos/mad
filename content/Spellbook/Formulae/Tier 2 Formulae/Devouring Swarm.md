# Devouring Swarm \[Formulae\] \[Create\] \[Nature\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Create 1, Nature 1
1 action. 2 Weave. 1 success. [[Delay]] 1. [[Cooldown]] 5.
[[Area Burst]] 1 [[Zone]] within 10/20.
Effect: Creatures that enter the Zone take 5 Damage (Pierce 4).
In the Autonomous phase, you may move the Zone up to 6 squares. When the Zone moves onto a creature, they take damage as if they entered it.
At the end of the Autonomous phase, you must pay 1 Weave or the Zone ends.

---
# Upgrades
## Rank 2
When you use this Formulae, either decrease the Delay by 1 or increase the amount you may move the Zone by 2 squares.
## Rank 3
Choose 1 upgrade:
* Area Burst 2 instead of 1.
* Two Area Burst 1’s.

# Arcane Sortie \[Formulae\] \[Enchantment\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Enchantment 1
1 action. 4 Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 4.
Effect: Make a [[Melee Attack]] with the following changes: You must move in a straight line, and the Damage is applied to all creatures that come within your Weapon range.

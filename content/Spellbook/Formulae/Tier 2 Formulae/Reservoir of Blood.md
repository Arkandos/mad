# Reservoir of Blood \[Formulae\] \[Control\] \[Blood\]
Tier 2. Rank 1. Prerequisite: Control 1, Blood 1.
1 action. X Weave. 1 success. [[Cooldown]] 5.
Effect: You spend up to 5 Weave, and gain twice that number of [[Blood Points]] until you use this Formulae again.

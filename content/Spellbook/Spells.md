Spells are the main way your mage interacts with the game, by crafting an effect on the fly from the [[#Techniques]] and [[#Forms]] you possess. There's no limit on the number of spell components a Mage may possess, but you should take care to not spread yourself too thin.
# Casting a Spell
When casting a Spell, you need to decide on all of its parts. A Spell requires at least one [[Opener]], and one [[Link]]. A Spell can only have one Opener, and a number of Links depending on the Opener (but at least one). You can also add a single [[Surge]] to a Spell, but that ends it and no further parts can be added.

All part of a Spell has the same target as the previous part. If targeting a creature beyond the first range but within the second range, you need to make a Power [[Core Rules|Test]] before resolving the effect. If you fail all targets take half damage or regain half as much Toughness, and ignore any other effect.
# Spell Costs
When you cast a Spell, you need to pay its total cost. Each part may add a cost like actions or Weave, and the entire cost must be paid in full. 
# Spell Resolution
If there is a question in which order a Spells parts resolve, it happens in the following order:
1. The Opener always happens first.
2. All Links, in the order chosen by the mage.
3. The Surge, if any.

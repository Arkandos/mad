Every time you finish a Mission, you gain experience points (XP) and Affinity ranks to advance your Mage for the challenges to come.

Between Missions, you can spend XP to acquire additional Techniques, Forms, Formulae, and Perks. You can also spend XP to upgrade those you already possess. You must fulfil all prerequisites to be able to acquire something.
# Affinities
Some missions allow you to increase a number of Affinity Ranks. This is as simple as increasing their numerical value. You can acquire as many different Affinities as you wish.
# [[Spells|Techniques and Forms]]
Acquiring a Technique or Form costs XP equal to its Tier (or 1 XP for Tier 0).

Upgrading costs XP equal to its Tier + its new Rank. That means that going from Tier 1 Rank 1 to Rank 2 would cost 3 XP (1 + 2). To then upgrade to Rank 3 would cost 4 XP (1 + 3)
# [[Formulae]]
Acquiring Formulae costs XP equal to its Tier (or 1 XP for Tier 0). Upgrading costs XP equal to its Tier + its new Rank, so going from Tier 1 Rank 1 to Rank 2 would cost 3 XP (1+2). To then upgrade to Rank 3 would cost 4 XP (1+3).
# [[Perks]]
Perks are permanent upgrades to your Mage that provide ongoing benefits. To acquire a Perk costs XP equal to its XP cost.

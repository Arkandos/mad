$$
Resist = 1 + (Mythos / 2) rounded up
$$
Whenever a negative effect like Acid or Fire would be applied to you by a non-player effect, you make a Resist roll. Each success reduces the numerical value of a status effect by 1 (your choice). If multiple statuses are applies to your simultaneously, you only make 1 Resist roll and decide which effects to reduce.

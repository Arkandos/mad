$$
Toughness = 10 + Might
$$
Toughness is how healthy your mage is. When you suffer damage, reduce your current toughness by that amount (after applying [[Armor]]). Once your Toughness reaches 0, you die. But do not worry, [[Dying|death is not the end.]]

Your Armor value is increased by the [[Armor (equipment)]] you wear. When you suffer damage, roll a number of dice equal to your Armor value. Each success reduces the damage by one. Then reduce your [[Toughness]] by that amount of damage.

\* When playing at Hard difficulty, you also suffer an Injury when you take damage that reduces you to 10 toughness or below. If one damage instance would reduce you from above 10 to 5 or below, you receive 2 Injuries.

```
Weave capacity: 10
```
Weave is the main resource that you spend in the game. It fuels almost every single ability, and is vital to manage carefully. At the start of every [[Core Rules#1. Mage Phase|Mage phase]], you regain 3 Weave.

If you need more Weave than that, you can take the [[Channel]] action to regain additional Weave.

Each player creates their own Mage. Together the mages form a Cabal, which is the name for your group of mages.
# Skills
Mages have 4 different skills, that are used in different ways.
- Might (*physical strength, toughness, melee attacks*)
	- Each point of Might increases your [[Toughness]] by 1.
- Power (*Dexterous coordination, speed, casting distance*)
	- Each point of Power increases your [[Speed]]
- Intelligence (*Critical thinking, social skills, channeling*)
	- Each point of Intelligence increases the amount of [[Weave]] gained when [[Channel|Channelling]]
- Mythos (*Resisting the enemy, luck, overcoming the odds*)
	- At the start of each mission, you gain Mythos points equal to your Mythos. You may spend 1 Mythos point to reroll all your dice in a skill or damage roll. You can only do this 1 / roll.
# Statistics
This is how the various statistics of your Mage are calculated

| Statistic                       | Value                            |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| [[Armor]]                       | 0                                |
| [[Toughness]]                   | 10 + Might                       |
| [[Weave]] capacity              | 10                               |
| [[Speed]]                       | 3 + half Power rounded up        |
| [[Resist]]                      | 1 + half Mythos rounded up       |
| [[Channel\|Channelling]] amount | 3 + half Intelligence rounded up |

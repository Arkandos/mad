Affinities are your Mage’s mystical attunements to the different types of magical arts. They enable you to unlock relevant [[Perks]] and [[Formulae]].
- Create
	  - Create new objects, bring elements into existence
- Control
	- Control existing objects and matter
- Enchantment
	- Modify existing creatures and objects
- Ward
	- Protect against outside forces
- Destroy
	  - Destroy and debilitate
- Perceive
	  - Enhance your senses and detect that which is not seen
- Rune
	  - Lingering effects on the ground
- Transmute
	  - Change one thing into another

- Air
	  - Control air, thunder, lightning and weather
- Earth
	  - Control earth, metal, weight
- Fire
	  - Control heat, cold, light
- Water
	  - Control water, ice, liquids
- Nature
	  - Control plants, animals
- Illusion
	  - Change how something is perceived, create false sensations
- Blood
	  - Uses your own or others lifeforce to power the magic
- Body
	  - Control creatures body, heal them
- Mind
	  - Control creatures mind
- Esoteric
	  - Manipulate and change magic
